# Episode 3: ONE BAG. THEY SAID ONE BAG.

**Setting:** London flat → Heathrow Terminal departures → departure gate

## Script

**Bounce:** Dash. I have one suitcase. One! Very reasonable. Very calm. Very me.
**Dash:** ...Why is it shaped like a pyramid?
**Bounce:** It's just clothes. And Aunty Bisi's shea butter. And the biscuits she specifically said to bring. And a few—
**Dash:** Bounce. The scale says thirty-eight kilos.
**Bounce:** The scale is lying and I won't hear otherwise.
**Bounce:** Sir it's mostly air in there— soft air— culturally significant air—
**Dash:** The screen says sixty-five pounds. Per. Bag.
**Bounce:** SIXTY FIVE? For LUGGAGE? They are taxing my AUNTY'S BISCUITS?!
**Dash:** Or... we could have sent Aunty Bisi's parcel ahead with BootHop. Cheaper than this fee. Arrives before you do.
**Bounce:** ...BootHop ships to Lagos?
**Dash:** Book the courier, split the parcel off, and your bag drops under the limit. Done in two minutes.
**Bounce:** I have been carrying shame in suitcase form my entire adult life and this was always the answer?!
**Bounce:** The biscuits will arrive before us. Aunty Bisi will never know.
**Dash:** She's going to ask why the tin is half empty.
**Bounce:** I was nervous! Snacking is a coping mechanism!

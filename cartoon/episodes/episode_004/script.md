# Episode 4: PASSPORT WAHALA: LONDON TO NAIJA EXPRESS

**Setting:** London consular pop-up venue, post office, London street pharmacy, Heathrow Airport departures

## Script

**Bounce:** Dash. DASH. My passport renewal is TODAY and I've got seventeen things to bring to Lagos, a £20 postal order to find, and Auntie's wig is STILL in a Lidl bag!
**Dash:** Good morning to you too. I've had my coffee. Have you?
**Bounce:** The Nigeria Immigration Service intervention is TODAY in London — forms, old passport, appointment slip, self-addressed envelope — and I printed everything on the wrong paper size!
**Dash:** A3. You printed a passport form on A3. That is… genuinely impressive, Bounce.
**Bounce:** AND Mum texted — she wants chin-chin, a bottle of Bigi Cola, two bags of Indomie and quote: 'don't forget my THING.' What thing?! She never said what thing!
**Bounce:** The queue — Dash — THE QUEUE. It goes around the building. Twice. A man brought a camping chair. A woman has a flask of egusi soup. This is not a queue, this is a SETTLEMENT.
**Guest:** Number four hundred and thirty-seven? …No? …Four hundred and thirty-eight? …My friend, the queue does not rush. The queue has been here since 1999.
**Bounce:** Four hundred and THIRTY-SEVEN?! I am number four hundred and EIGHTY-TWO!
**Guest:** Young mouse. Your friend is making a scene. In my experience… the organised man does not queue. He arrives prepared.
**Dash:** Right. Bounce. Stop. Breathe. BootHop. I've already pre-sorted your parcel collection, booked the post office slot for the postal order, and your documents are reprinted — A4 this time — in the BootHop app. Everything's tracked, timed and ready.
**Bounce:** …You did all of that while I was catastrophising?
**Bounce:** Documents submitted. Parcel collected. Postal order DONE. Auntie's wig — re-packed properly, not in a Lidl bag. And Mum's 'thing'… turned out to be a specific brand of Tura soap. I found it. I found the soap, Dash.
**Dash:** BootHop had a pharmacy partner two streets away. I added it to the route forty minutes ago.
**Bounce:** You know what, for a tiny mouse, you are enormous where it counts.
**Bounce:** Next time I'll start the passport renewal before the actual intervention squad has to fly in from Abuja to save me.
**Dash:** I'll put a reminder in BootHop. Four reminders. With escalating alarm sounds.

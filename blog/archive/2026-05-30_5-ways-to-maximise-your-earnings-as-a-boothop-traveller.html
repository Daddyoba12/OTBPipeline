5 Earner Tips

1. List early — post your journey as soon as you book your flight. Senders plan ahead. The earlier you're listed, the more matches you'll get.

2. Set a competitive rate — check what other travellers on your route are charging. Being £2–£3/kg lower than average wins you bookings quickly when you're building your reputation.

3. Offer extra kg if you can — if you have two bags or a generous allowance, advertise it. Senders with heavier parcels specifically look for high-capacity travellers.

4. Complete KYC immediately — unverified travellers get fewer matches. KYC takes 3 minutes. Do it the day you register.

5. Build your rating — after every successful delivery, your rating goes up. A 5-star rated traveller earns 20–30% more than a new profile because senders filter by trust score.

The Consistency Play

Most top earners on BootHop travel 3–5 times a year and carry items on every trip. At £150 per trip average, that's £450–£750 per year — from space that would have been empty.

The travellers who earn most are the ones who treat it like a side hustle: plan, communicate promptly, deliver professionally and collect great ratings.

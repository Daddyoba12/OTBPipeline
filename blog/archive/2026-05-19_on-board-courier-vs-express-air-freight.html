What is an On-Board Courier (OBC)?

An On-Board Courier service, often referred to as "Hand Carry," involves a dedicated person who physically accompanies your shipment from the point of origin to the final destination. Instead of moving through a mechanical sorting facility, the item stays with the courier as cabin baggage or checked luggage on a commercial flight.

Key Characteristics of OBC:

  • Human-Escorted: Your shipment is never left unattended in a warehouse.
  • Direct Routing: The courier takes the next available commercial flight, bypassing the "hub-and-spoke" delays of traditional cargo networks.
  • Rapid Customs: Because the item travels with a passenger, it can often be cleared through customs more quickly as "accompanied baggage," provided all regulatory paperwork is in order.

What is Express Air Freight?

Express Air Freight is the premium tier of traditional logistics. Companies like DHL, FedEx, and UPS operate massive global networks where shipments are consolidated, sorted at central hubs, and flown on dedicated cargo planes.

Key Characteristics of Express Air Freight:

  • Network-Driven: Shipments move through a standardised system of trucks, sorting centres, and planes.
  • High Capacity: Better suited for larger, bulkier shipments that exceed the weight limits of a single traveller.
  • Standardised Security: Relies on X-ray screening and automated tracking numbers.

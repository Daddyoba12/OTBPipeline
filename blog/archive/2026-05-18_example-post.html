Every week, thousands of people across the UK need to send something to Nigeria. A birthday gift. A document. Medicine for a parent. Something irreplaceable.

The traditional options are expensive, slow, and impersonal. DHL quotes three hundred pounds. The parcel takes three weeks. Nobody can tell you where it is.

BootHop was built to change that — by connecting senders with real people already making the journey.

How It Works

A Booter posts their travel route — London to Lagos, Manchester to Abuja. A Hooper posts what they need to send. BootHop matches them automatically based on route, date, and price.

Both parties verify their identity. Payment is held securely until delivery is confirmed by both sides. Nobody gets paid until the goods arrive.

Built on Compliance

Every item is screened through our AI-powered customs compliance engine before a match is ever made. Users know upfront whether their item can be carried, what documents are needed, and what duties apply at the destination.

No surprises at the border. No seized packages. No grey areas.

The Result

Senders pay a fraction of courier costs. Travellers earn from empty suitcase space on journeys they were already making. BootHop earns a small platform fee — only on successful deliveries.

Simple. Safe. Same day.

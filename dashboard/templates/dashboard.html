How the BootHop Pipeline Works

Your pipeline runs daily, creating professional social media content for your brand — automatically. Here's exactly what happens and how to use each feature.

1
The Daily Content Pipeline

🎬
4 content slots per day
Your pipeline generates up to 4 pieces of content daily — Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and a Weekly deep-dive. Each slot gets a unique video, script, and caption tailored to your brand.
🤖
AI writes the script and hook
Claude AI generates a short, punchy script based on your business, industry, and target audience. It rotates through content angles — awareness, social proof, tips, and offers — so posts never feel repetitive.
🎵
Music and voiceover added automatically
Each video gets background music and a professional AI voiceover narrating the hook. The final video is ready to post — no editing needed.
📱
Posted to your platforms
Once approved (or on auto-post), the video goes to TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube — whichever platforms you've connected. You get a Telegram notification with a preview when each one posts.
Pipeline runs
Script written, video rendered, voiceover added
You get a Telegram preview
Review the video — approve, reject, or re-voice
Posted to all platforms
Confirmation sent back to Telegram with platform links

2
Pipeline Control Tab

📊
See your slot status at a glance
Each slot card shows the current status — Pending means it's scheduled, Posted means it went out. Expand a card to see the script, hook, and which platforms received it.
▶️
Run a slot manually
Hit Run Now on any slot to trigger it immediately — useful if the scheduled run missed or you want to push content at a specific time.
📅
Schedule control
The pipeline schedule shows the next post times for each slot. Contact BootHop to adjust your posting times.

3
Revoice Studio

🎤
What is Revoice Studio?
Revoice Studio lets you swap the voiceover on any pipeline video — using AI text-to-speech or your own recorded voice — without re-running the whole pipeline. Great for personalising content or correcting a script.
✍️
Option A — AI voice (easiest)
1. Pick a slot video from the dropdown (or upload your own)
2. Type or paste new script text in the box
3. Choose a voice (Alloy, Nova, Echo, Onyx…)
4. Hit Generate Audio — listen to the preview
5. Hit Bake Video — the new audio is merged into the video
6. Download or it's sent straight to Telegram
🎙️
Option B — Your own voice
Upload a .mp3 or .wav recording from your phone or laptop instead of using AI. Same bake process — your voice, merged with the pipeline video.
Tip: To quickly revoice today's video — go to Pipeline Control, find the slot, and tap the Revoice button. It pre-loads that slot's video into Revoice Studio automatically.

4
Frequently Asked Questions

Why didn't my slot post today? ▾
The most common reasons: the pipeline schedule hasn't reached that slot time yet, or the slot ran but a platform credential expired. Check the slot status card — if it shows an error, contact BootHop. You can also hit Run Now to trigger it manually.
Can I change the script before the video posts? ▾
Yes — use Revoice Studio to regenerate the audio with a new script before posting. The pipeline won't auto-post until you run the slot or it hits its scheduled time, giving you a window to review and revoice.
How do I get my Telegram notifications? ▾
Your pipeline sends video previews and post confirmations to the Telegram chat ID registered on your account. Make sure you've started a chat with the BootHop bot — message it once to activate it. If you're not receiving messages, contact BootHop to verify your chat ID.
What if I want to pause or skip a day? ▾
Contact BootHop directly — we can pause your schedule for specific dates or set slots to manual-only so nothing posts without your approval.
How do I change my password? ▾
Contact BootHop and we'll reset it for you. A self-service password reset is coming soon.
Who do I contact for help? ▾
Reach out via Telegram to your BootHop account manager, or email support@boothop.com. We typically respond within a few hours.

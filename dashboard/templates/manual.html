Client Guide
Everything you need to know about your BootHop Pipeline — from logging in to reading your Telegram notifications.

What is BootHop Pipeline?

BootHop Pipeline is your automated social media content system. Once it is set up and switched on, it works every day without you needing to do anything. It finds trending topics relevant to your business, writes a short video script, assembles a branded video, and posts it to your chosen social media platforms — automatically, on the schedule you agreed with the BootHop team.

Your client dashboard is where you can see everything that has been posted, re-do a video if you want different wording, and manage your account settings.

Good to know
Nothing in your pipeline requires you to take action each day. It runs in the background. You only need to log in if you want to check on posts, make a change, or adjust your password.

Logging In

Your login page is at:

boothop.com/pipeline-login

You will need two things to sign in:

Company IDA short name your account was set up with, for example: d818 or g-inspired. The BootHop team gave you this when your pipeline was activated.
PasswordThe password you set up, or the temporary one the BootHop team gave you when your account was created. If you have not changed it yet, do so now — Settings tab → Change Password.

Forgot your password?

Go to boothop.com/forgot-password and enter your Company ID and the email address on your account. A reset link will be sent to your email and to your Telegram (if you have that set up). Click the link and choose a new password. The link expires after one hour.

Tip
Save boothop.com/pipeline-login as a bookmark on your phone. It works on mobile as well as desktop.

Your Dashboard

Once you log in you will see your dashboard. It has four tabs along the top:

Pipeline Control Tab

This is your main activity feed. Every time your pipeline runs a slot, a new entry appears here.

ColumnWhat it means
HookThe opening line of the video — the sentence designed to grab attention in the first 2 seconds.
PlatformsWhich social media accounts the video was posted to.
TimeWhen the pipeline ran for that slot.
StatusPosted = successfully published. Pending = being processed. Failed = something went wrong — the BootHop team is notified automatically.
About your schedule
Your pipeline runs at the slot times agreed during your onboarding call. For example, 09:00, 13:00, and 18:00 in your local time. You will receive a Telegram message each time a slot runs.

Revoice Studio Tab

Sometimes a video is generated on a topic that is fine but you want the wording changed, or the voice to sound different. Revoice lets you do this without rerunning the whole pipeline.

1

Choose a video

Go to the Pipeline Control tab and click Revoice on any row. You will be taken to the Revoice Studio.

2

Edit the script

Change any of the words that will be spoken in the video. Keep it under about 90 seconds when read aloud.

3

Choose a voice

Pick the tone — calm, energetic, or authoritative — depending on the content.

4

Click Revoice

The new audio is applied to the video and it is re-rendered. The original version is always kept in your history.

Settings Tab

Change your password

Go to the Settings tab. You will see a Change Password form. Enter your current password, then your new password twice, and click Update Password. Your new password takes effect immediately.

Important
Passwords must be at least 8 characters. Do not share your password with anyone. The BootHop team will never ask for your password.

Your account details

The Settings tab also shows your Company ID, registered email address, and login URL for easy reference.

Setting Up Telegram

Telegram is a free messaging app that your pipeline uses to send you notifications — for example, when a video is posted, or when your password is reset. You do not need to use Telegram for anything else. Setting it up takes about 3 minutes.

Why Telegram and not WhatsApp?
Telegram allows automated bots to send messages directly — it is free and instant. WhatsApp Business API requires a monthly fee and business verification. We use Telegram for pipeline notifications and WhatsApp optionally for direct contact with the BootHop team.

Step-by-step: Set up Telegram and get your Chat ID

1

Download the app

Search for Telegram in the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android). It is free. Install it.

2

Create your account

Open Telegram and tap Start Messaging. Enter your phone number. You will receive a code by SMS — enter it to confirm. Choose a display name.

3

Find the userinfobot

In Telegram, tap the Search icon (magnifying glass at the top). Type @userinfobot and tap on the result that appears.

4

Send any message

Tap the chat with @userinfobot and type anything — for example just hi — and press Send. The bot will instantly reply with your account details.

5

Copy your Chat ID

The bot's reply will include a line like: Id: 8641867751. That number is your Chat ID. Copy it.

6

Share it with the BootHop team

Send your Chat ID to the BootHop team via email or WhatsApp. They will add it to your pipeline account so notifications start coming through.

Once it is set up
You will receive a Telegram message from @BoothHopBot each time your pipeline posts a video. The message will show the hook line and which platforms it was posted to.

Setting Up WhatsApp Business

WhatsApp Business is a free app (separate from the normal WhatsApp) designed for small businesses. It lets you show your business name, address, and hours to people who message you. The BootHop team may use WhatsApp to send you updates or check in — this is optional but recommended.

1

Download WhatsApp Business

Search for WhatsApp Business in the App Store or Google Play. It is a separate, free app — not the same as the regular WhatsApp. Install it.

2

Register with your business number

Open the app and enter a phone number. This should be your business phone number. You will receive a verification code by SMS or call.

3

Set up your business profile

Tap More Options (three dots) → Settings → Business tools → Business profile. Fill in your business name, category, description, address, website, and email.

4

Share your number with BootHop

Send your WhatsApp Business number to the BootHop team. They can then reach you directly if anything needs your attention.

Tip
You can run WhatsApp Business on the same phone as your regular WhatsApp — they are separate apps and use different numbers (or the same number if you want to switch).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to do anything each day?

No. The pipeline runs automatically at your agreed schedule. You will just receive Telegram notifications telling you what was posted. You only need to log in if you want to check something or make a change.

What if I want to approve a video before it goes live?

By default, the pipeline posts automatically without waiting for approval — this is what makes it hands-free. If you want to review videos first, contact the BootHop team and they can discuss options for your account.

Can I stop the pipeline temporarily?

Yes. Contact the BootHop team via Telegram or WhatsApp and they will pause your pipeline immediately. Self-service pause is coming soon.

What happens if a post fails?

You will receive a Telegram message alerting you. The BootHop team is also notified automatically and will investigate. Most failures are caused by an expired platform token and are fixed the same day.

Can I upload my own video clips?

Yes. Send your video files (MP4 or MOV format, minimum 5 seconds) to the BootHop team and they will add them to your personal clip library. Your clips will always be used before any stock footage.

How do I change my posting schedule?

Contact the BootHop team. They will update your schedule in the system and in cron.org. You can request different times, fewer days, or a different timezone if your business situation changes.

Is my social media login safe?

Yes. We never store your social media username or password. We use API access tokens issued by each platform. These can be revoked at any time from your platform's security settings — we have no ongoing access to your account beyond what the token permits.

I forgot my password and do not have Telegram yet

Go to boothop.com/forgot-password. The reset link will be sent to your registered email address as well as Telegram. Check your email inbox (and spam folder). If you still cannot get in, contact the BootHop team directly.
